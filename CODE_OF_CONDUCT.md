# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to creating a welcoming, friendly, and respectful environment for everyone.  
By participating in this project, you agree to maintain a harassment-free experience for all contributors.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behaviour include:
- Being respectful and inclusive
- Giving constructive feedback
- Being open to different ideas and viewpoints
- Showing empathy toward others

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Posting offensive or inappropriate content
- Personal attacks or threats
- Discriminatory language or behaviour
- Disruptive or intentionally harmful actions

## Our Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Reviewing and merging contributions fairly
- Clarifying standards and expectations
- Taking action when unacceptable behaviour occurs
- Removing or rejecting inappropriate contributions

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces
- Public interactions related to the project, both online and offline

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the project maintainer at:

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly.

## Consequences
Project maintainers may take actions such as:
- Warning the offender
- Removing inappropriate content
- Temporarily or permanently banning contributors who violate the rules
